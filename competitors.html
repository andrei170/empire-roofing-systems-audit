You have the bigger footprint and the smaller signal

These are much smaller operations than yours on every measure we can see — one office, five to fifteen pages. But they have done the one cheap thing that tells a search engine where they work, and you have not.

You run three offices and not one page on your website says so in its address. That is a fixable gap, and it is fixable for all three cities at once.

The Open Lane
Why the empty careers column is the most valuable thing on this page
  • Every firm here is competing for the same roofers and none of them is asking. The hiring market in this trade runs almost entirely on who knows whom. That works until you need nine to twenty-four people, which is exactly where you are.
  • Recruitment advertising is cheap because nobody bids on it. Customer keywords in roofing are expensive and crowded. "Flat roofer jobs Kettering" is neither.
  • You have the best story to tell and it is not close. NFRC membership, guarantees to thirty years, commercial and industrial contracts, three regions of work, manufacturer-approved systems. To a roofer choosing an employer that reads as stability and standards.
  • Three offices is a recruitment advantage, not just a sales one. Work in one region going quiet does not mean laying people off. That is a genuinely attractive thing to say to somebody deciding whether to leave a smaller firm.
  • Your own drone and job photography is already the right content. A roofer wants to see the kind of roofs they would be standing on. You have those pictures; they are just filed under "Gallery" rather than aimed at anyone.
The Second Open Lane
Not one firm here sells planned maintenance properly

None of the four sites we looked at, yours included, has a page explaining a flat roof maintenance package — what is inspected, how often, what is included, what it protects.

This matters specifically for a business about to add nine to twenty-four people. Maintenance work is scheduled, recurring and predictable. One-off installations are not. Predictable work is what makes a bigger payroll survivable in a trade where January is not August.

You already say "Maintenance Packages Available" — as the last bullet of a list, with no page behind it. Everybody else says nothing at all.

What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Actual search rankings. No rank tracker has been run against any of these firms. The page-address comparison is structural evidence, not a ranking report.
  • Google Business Profiles and review counts. Not compared for any firm here, yours included. In roofing the map pack often produces more enquiries than the website does.
  • Whether these firms advertise vacancies elsewhere. We checked their own websites. Indeed, Facebook, agencies and word of mouth were not assessed, and several of them almost certainly use some of those. The finding is that nothing on their own sites addresses recruitment — which is where somebody checks you out before applying.
  • Their headcount, turnover, traffic or results. We have seen public websites and nothing else, and we make no claim about any of it.
  • Your own analytics. Our automated request to your site was refused, so we could not inspect what tracking you have. No claim is made either way.
  • Whether other larger contractors compete with you on commercial tenders. This analysis covers the local flat roofing firms visible around Kettering. National commercial roofing contractors were not assessed and are a different competitive set.

Four firms, four empty careers pages, and up to twenty-four people to find.

The lane is open. It stays open until one of you walks into it.
